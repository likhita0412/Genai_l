What to look for
Compare the four outputs and note:
* Vague vs clear: does specifying the count and format improve the output?
* Few-shot: does the model copy your example format closely? Showing an example is often more effective than describing the format.
* Role + steps: does breaking the task into steps (ideas first, then questions) improve quality?
* Does the 3B model ever invent facts that aren’t in the text? That’s why “based only on the text below” matters.
